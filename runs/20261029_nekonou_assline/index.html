In-game Time (IGT) 3D Progression Timelapse

Limitations
  • Placements that a rollback undid are left out.
  • doesn't log breaks, so a broken block stays drawn.
  • doesn't log pipe connections. A pipe is drawn joined to every neighboring machine, and to each neighboring pipe of the same kind.
  • doesn't log the facing of single-block machines, hatches and buses, so their front is drawn on all four sides.
  • A multiblock controller with a footprint logged by audit mod 0.8.0 or later faces the logged direction.
  • doesn't log player facing or collect timed position logs. Positions shown here happen during inventory snapshots.
